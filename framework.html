SECTION 2 — DEFINE TODAY’S PLAYGROUND Opening Box + volatility reference

Step 1 — Draw the Opening Box

Box

At 9:10:

Mark:

  • The highest price from 9:00–9:10
  • The lowest price from 9:00–9:10

This is your box.

From now on, all decisions relate to:

  • The top of the box
  • The bottom of the box

The box is your reference structure.

Step 2 — Measure Today’s Energy

Volatility

Take the total box range.

Divide by five.

This is your volatility reference for today.

You will use this number to judge:

  • Breakout strength
  • Pullback depth
  • Overextension
  • Stop size
  • Continuation strength
No-trade filter

If the total opening box is less than 140:

→ The morning lacks expansion.

→ You do not trade today.

Quiet open = low probability breakout environment.

SECTION 3 — DECIDE WHAT KIND OF MORNING THIS IS Choose Range Morning or Trend Morning (no mixing)

Decision — Range Morning or Trend Morning?

Mode

You must choose:

Is this a Range Morning,
or a Trend Morning?

You cannot trade both ideas simultaneously.

RANGE MORNING

Read the tape

Price behavior usually shows:

  • Rotation inside the box
  • Frequent returns back toward the middle
  • Expansion outside the box struggles to exceed about 50% of volatility reference
  • Breakouts fade quickly
  • Direction flips easily

If that behavior dominates → RANGE MODE.

Authority
In Range Mode, the box edge is authority.
It is the line the market must prove it can hold.

TREND MORNING

Read the tape

Price behavior usually shows:

  • Expansion clearly outside the box
  • Movement exceeding about 50% of volatility reference that does NOT collapse back
  • Pullbacks that are controlled and do not exceed about 60% of volatility reference
  • Clear directional structure forming

If that behavior dominates → TREND MODE.

Authority
In Trend Mode, structure replaces the box as authority.
Uncertainty rule

If uncertain → reduce size or stand aside.

Ambiguous structure produces low-quality trades.

Make this decision calmly.

Do not force one.

RANGE MODE Reverse-First Breakout execution

Step R1 — A Breakout Happens

R1

Two possibilities:

  • Price closes above the box
  • Price closes below the box

That alone is not enough.

Step R2 — Breakout Must Be Meaningful

R2
Allowed

Close exceeds the box by at least 20% of today’s volatility reference.

This shows commitment.

Not allowed
  • Wick-only breakout
  • Micro close outside the box
  • Immediate fade

Weak breakouts are traps.

Step R3 — Reverse-First Requirement

R3

After breakout:

You wait.

Allowed
  • Price pulls back toward the edge
  • Pullback retraces at least 35% of volatility reference

This confirms the move is not emotional.

Not allowed
  • No pullback
  • Straight vertical continuation
Chase rule
If there is no pullback, you missed it.
You do not chase.

Step R4 — Tight Retest Behavior

R4

During the pullback:

Allowed
  • Pullback remains near the edge
  • It stays within about 35% of volatility reference

This means the edge is holding.

Not allowed
  • Pullback goes deep inside the box
  • Pullback becomes wide and unstable

Reset rule:

If price moves away more than about
60% of volatility reference
from the edge
before forming a proper retest:

→ Treat the breakout structure as expired.

This does NOT mean the market is banned.

It means that specific structure has dissolved.

You may trade again only if a new clean structure forms:

  • New swing
  • New controlled pullback
  • Fresh confirmation

Structure must remain clean.

Step R5 — Hold and Turn (Final Permission)

R5

You now need to see:

  • Pullback slows
  • Selling pressure fades (for long)
  • Buying pressure fades (for short)
  • Small reversal begins

Entry allowed only when:

Price resumes with about
25% of volatility reference
in breakout direction.

Identity
You do not enter at the edge.
You enter at confirmation.

Step R6 — Skip Runaway Moves

R6

If price extends more than about
110% of volatility reference
before forming proper pullback:

→ Skip the trade.

The move has already released its energy.

No chase
You do not chase expansion.
You wait for new structure.

Step R7 — Failed Breakout

R7

If price breaks out and returns inside the box:

→ Breakout failed.

Action:

  • No breakout trades for 12 minutes

If it flips and breaks the other side quickly:

→ Market unstable.

Action:

  • No breakout trades for 6 minutes

Chaos must cool down.
